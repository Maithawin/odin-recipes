Pasta

Description

A simple and deliciuos pasta dish that is easy to make and perfect for a quiick meal.

A tasty and easy pasta dish made with simple Ingredients

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • tomato sauce
  • Garlic
  • salt
  • cheese

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta in the water.
  2. Cook the garlic in a pan.
  3. Add the tomato sauce, salt, and cheese.
  4. Add the cooked pasta and mix well.
  5. Serve and enjoy
