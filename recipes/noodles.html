Noodles

Description

These noodles are quick, simple, and deliciuos. They are perfect for tasty meal when you want something easy to prepare.

The noodles can be coocked in multiple ways.

Ingredients

  • Noodles
  • Water
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Boil the noodels in the water until they are cooked.
  2. Drain the noodles.
  3. Add cheese and mix until it melts.
  4. Add salt to taste and serve.
